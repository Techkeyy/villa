THE OPERATOR PROBLEM

Event Contract markets need liquidity that can keep up.

DreamDEX markets are more useful when traders can find a price. For the liquidity provider, that means repeatedly deciding what a contract is worth, how much exposure is acceptable, when to stop quoting, and what to do after a fill or expiry.

Empty or thin booksManual quote decisionsLifecycle work after every window

A CLEARER LIQUIDITY WORKFLOW

The product for the person supplying the liquidity.

VILLA turns pricing, risk checks, quoting, rollover, settlement, and account visibility into a workflow an operator can inspect. You decide how much capital to make available, and your connected wallet remains the authority over it.

WHAT VILLA DOES

From a live BTC market to a clean settlement state.

Every step is tied to the exact market and the account that owns the liquidity.

01

Prices independently

VILLA forms a BTC Event Contract value from the underlying, reference, time, and realized volatility.

02

Checks the risk

The Risk Governor decides whether new exposure is allowed, should reduce, or must halt.

03

Posts maker liquidity

Quotes are planned on the venue grid and remain post-only, so the workflow does not cross the book.

04

Reacts to fills

Inventory and pending orders are checked together so the next action reflects what is actually at risk.

05

Rolls to the next market

When a window leaves Trading, VILLA closes that scope and looks for the next compatible BTC market.

06

Tracks settlement

It keeps market identity, claims, redemption, and final wallet hygiene in the lifecycle record.

HOW IT WORKS

A short path from capital to monitored liquidity.

Each LP gets an owner-scoped account. The connected wallet approves every capital action, while execution stays disabled in this phase.

RISK AND SAFETY

Automation is useful only when stopping is clear.

VILLA does not promise profitability. It makes safety decisions explicit and fails closed when important facts are missing.

Risk Governor

ALLOW, REDUCE_ONLY, and HALT are policy outcomes, not marketing labels.

Stop controls

STOP, pause, and cancel-all pathways are designed to prevent new exposure before cleanup.

Account boundary

The LP account is scoped to its owner, market, operator, and tracked orders. Withdrawals stay owner-only.

Honest status

Unavailable PnL, pending proof, and replay evidence stay labelled instead of being filled with assumptions.
