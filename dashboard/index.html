A CLEARER LIQUIDITY WORKFLOW

Market-making without turning the LP journey into a terminal.

VILLA is designed for people providing liquidity to Event Contracts. It turns the work of pricing, quoting, risk checks, market rollover, and settlement into a product you can understand and monitor.

HOW IT WORKS

A short path from capital to monitored liquidity.

The capital path is being built around owner-scoped accounts. Preview controls remain inactive until that boundary is proven.

01

Add liquidity

Choose how much of your own capital you want to make available.

02

VILLA prices

VILLA calculates a fair value for the active Event Contract.

03

VILLA quotes

It places bounded maker liquidity when the market and risk rules allow it.

04

Manages risk

The Risk Governor can reduce, pause, or halt new risk when conditions change.

05

Settles and rolls

VILLA tracks the lifecycle and moves to the next short-duration market when ready.

WHY VILLA

The useful parts of automation, with the controls in view.

01

Independent pricing

VILLA brings its own fair-value view to the market instead of blindly copying the book.

02

Risk Governor

Configured checks bound exposure and can move the strategy to reduce-only or halt.

03

Automatic rollover

Lifecycle awareness helps VILLA stop quoting an old market and find the next one.

04

Capital-control model

The product is being designed so each LP keeps authority over their own capital.
