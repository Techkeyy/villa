01THE PROBLEM

A market needs more than a price on a screen.

DreamDEX Event Contracts need dependable maker liquidity so traders can enter and exit without waiting for a counterparty. For one operator, keeping that liquidity healthy means watching prices, exposure, fills, market windows, and settlement at the same time.

02WHAT VILLA DOES

It turns a difficult market routine into one visible loop.

Each part has a clear job. The operator can see what VILLA believes, what it is allowed to do, and what happened next.

  1. 01
    Prices the event

    Builds an independent BTC up or down estimate from the underlying market and time remaining.

  2. 02
    Checks the risk

    Tests data quality, balances, gas, exposure, and market conditions before a quote is allowed.

  3. 03
    Provides maker liquidity

    Places bounded post-only quotes when the safety checks allow it.

  4. 04
    Responds to change

    Reconciles fills, adjusts inventory, and moves safely when a market closes.

  5. 05
    Tracks the finish

    Records settlement, redemption, and the handoff to the next matching market.

04RISK AND SAFETY

The safe answer is sometimes no quote.

The Risk Governor can allow quoting, reduce exposure, or halt new risk. The console keeps that decision visible and gives the operator direct pause, stop, and cancel controls.

ALLOWNew maker liquidity may be considered.
REDUCE ONLYExisting directional risk can be reduced, not increased.
HALTNew quoting stops until the safety condition is resolved.

VILLA does not promise profit. Realized PnL stays unavailable until it can be measured honestly.
